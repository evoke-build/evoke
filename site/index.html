It starts with one reflex.

A reflex is a small program someone wrote, with a manifest anyone can read. The manifest says what it does, what it takes and how careful to be.

It runs on its own only when evoke is sure enough: 0.6 to look something up, 0.8 to change something. When it can't be undone, it asks a person, however sure it is.

It runs when it is sure enough for what the reflex changes. It asks when something is missing or unclear, and always before anything that can't be undone.

The rules

02

What holds at every size.

The same rules for one reflex on a laptop and a thousand across companies.

  1. Nothing is made up.Every value is a choice someone listed, a word you taught it, or a piece of what you typed.
  2. The plan comes first.You see every step, and where it runs, before any of them does.
  3. A number on every step.Each decision says how sure evoke is, and the bar rises with what the step would change.
  4. A person before the irreversible.Anything that can't be undone asks a person. No setting skips it.
  5. Lookups together, changes in turn.What only reads runs side by side. What changes waits its turn.
  6. Results fill, never steer.A result can fill in a later step. It can never tell that step what to do.
  7. Every machine keeps its gate.Each machine checks every call itself, and asks its own person.
  8. No center.Machines find each other through public addresses. Nobody sits in the middle, not even us.

What is new is the plan: not the app, not the flow, not the prompt.

The road

05

How we get there.

In order, each stop measured before the next one is promised. The first runs today.

  1. TodayOne sentence, a few reflexes. The plan first, lookups together, a person before what can't be undone.
  2. Trust at today's sizeA sandbox around every reflex. The confidence numbers measured. Six real problems pinned as tests.
  3. Plans people can holdPlans saved, reviewed and run later by someone else. A short sentence for a long reviewed plan. Plans that wait overnight for a yes.
  4. Many reflexes, many authorsHundreds installed at once. A second engine that runs on your machine. Reflexes from people you've never met.
  5. Everywhere people actApps that ship their reflexes. Chat and other places to ask. Agents that propose while evoke decides.
  6. Across machinesPlans that cross machines and companies, every step signed and every gate kept.

How we will know.

evoke sends nothing home. Every measure is one the tests take, or a person reports.

  • The six problems run green over real systems, for a team that is not us.
  • Wrong runs at or over the bar to change something, counted per thousand.
  • The plan chosen right at 20, 50, 100, 300 and a thousand reflexes installed.
  • Plans written by one person and run by another. Plans that waited at a gate overnight, then resumed.
  • Reflexes and applications shipped by people we have never met.

People write the programs. A sentence picks the plan. A person signs what can't be undone.
